gina-starter-pack

By Harry Winter · TypeScript · Source on GitHub

Three Polymarket negRisk strategy packs built as scheduled workflows for Ask Gina’s Predictions vertical. Each pack is a pipeline of scanners feeding a risk-gated executor that defaults to dry-run.

Pack 1: negRisk basket arbitrage (three layers)

  1. Scanner: checks the sum-of-YES invariant and depth-walks each flagged event at $50, $500 and $5,000, classifying gaps as real, marginal or trap.
  2. Volume-tier filter: groups events into flagship (≥$1M), mid and tail tiers by dollar volume.
  3. Maker executor: a risk gate (capital, position count, daily notional, daily-loss kill-switch) then maker-only limit orders, with basket convergence monitoring.

Pack 2 and Pack 3

  • Maker yield: a scanner that models yield under naive, moderate and informed adverse selection, feeding a two-sided quoting executor.
  • Favourite-longshot bias: a research study backtested on 215 resolved events and 3,319 constituents. No statistically significant edge survived, so it is marked research and dry-run only.

Testing

Pack 1 went through seven validation passes, including an adversarial red-team that found and fixed a SQL-injection bug, a live end-to-end run, and a pre-send sweep of the executor. The risk gate blocked all six deliberate bypass attempts in Pack 3.

Why maker-only

Depth-walking showed that a +300bp top-of-book gap shrank to +60bp at realistic size, and taker-side P&L per cycle was negative. Only passive, maker-side execution made economic sense.

Built with

TypeScript, Ask Gina workflow runtime, SQLite, Python, Polymarket CLOB API.
